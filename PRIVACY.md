# Privacy Policy

Effective: August 5, 2026

Unfurl is a macOS posture and menu-bar camera app. It collects no data. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

## Camera

The app uses your camera to show a live preview and to analyze your posture. All processing happens on device. Camera frames are never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. The app is sandboxed and has no network access, so it could not send them even if it wanted to.

## What is stored, and where

- Photos you explicitly take are saved to your Pictures folder. Nothing is captured without you pressing the button.
- Posture statistics and calibration are stored in a local file on your Mac.
- Settings are stored in the app's local preferences.

All of this stays on your Mac. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app and its data.

## What is not collected

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no advertising, no third-party services. The app makes no network connections at all.

## Notifications

Posture reminders are local notifications generated on your Mac. They involve no server.

## Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version ships.
